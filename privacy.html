Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy describes how Sorha Agency ("we") handles data in its internal e-mail tool, "Sorha Mail Bot", which uses the Gmail API.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by Sorha Agency, on Sorha's own Gmail accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

With our authorisation, the tool reads, creates and sends messages and drafts in Sorha's own Gmail mailboxes, in order to send collaboration proposals to content creators, read their replies and detect delivery failures.

Contacts

We write to creators using the professional contact e-mail they publish on their public social media profile. We keep the address, the public profile name and the history of our messages, only to manage the collaboration. We do not sell or share this data with third parties.

Google user data

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features above, is stored only on Sorha's own computer and Gmail accounts, and is not transferred to anyone else. Sorha Mail Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

If you no longer want to hear from us, or want your data removed, reply to our e-mail or write to sorhamarketing@gmail.com and we will remove it.
